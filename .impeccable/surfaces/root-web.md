# Token field: /@root/web/

## Scope and mode

Mode: Experience, with Operate controls for reading and flight. Primary target:
`src/msg/data/root-web.html`; related sources are the assembled `root-web*.js`
and `root-web*.css`. This is the current Root post universe. The former bright
introduction remains a historical brand reference in `DESIGN.md`.

## Confirmed direction — 2026-10-03

A deep-black token field, geometric clarity, sparse monochrome matter and
identity-seeded low-poly planets with distinct terrain. Near views show shaded
relief and closed raised crater rims. Depth comes from the world and camera,
with quiet fine-bordered overlays around it. `gadgets.muse.ai` informs only dark
monochrome material, fine borders and spatial interaction; no assets or layout
are copied. Ordinary Home and document views keep their existing reading theme.

## Visual semantics

The immutable root subject is the white geometric anchor with a layered corona.
Valid publicly readable certificates add modest size, tint, hexagonal seal and
diamond mark. Brightness separately follows expiring self-reported presence and
recent readable public posts. Missing evidence is unknown, not offline. Public
balance uses a thin segmented reserve arc, never body size; precise units and
configured currency appear on selection. Hidden and zero reserves are distinct.
Own private balance appears only in the session owner's private orbit.

Certificate graphics never confer permission or a reputation score. Decorative
token glyphs do not represent users. Labels, posts and amounts use text nodes.
The public avatar projection is bounded and readable under anonymous authority.
Avatar badges try eight directions around their planet, keeping their size and
distance; labels, other badges, HUD, panels and viewport edges take precedence.
If no position clears these obstacles, the decoration is hidden.

## Interaction and density

Orbit, pan and approach remain available outside flight. Search and the star
catalog provide keyboard/native alternatives. Intro hints and mission copy fade
after real interaction for the visit; `?` recovers help and progress. Mobile
selection frames the planet above the detail sheet. Reduced motion and Pause
stop ambient motion. Canvas projects the same 3D scene when WebGL is unavailable.

Flight uses independent single-axis controls: a vertical pitch stick on the
left, a horizontal yaw stick on the right, and separate thrust/brake buttons.
Pull pitch down to raise the nose; push up to lower it. Touch pointers are
independent so both sticks and an action can be held together. Release/cancel,
blur and page hiding clear held input. The sticks expose slider values and
keyboard alternatives; controls avoid search/composer typing.

Flight connects to the server-owned world. Position, collisions, hits, pickups,
health, fuel, cooldowns and respawn are server decisions. Local flashes show
requests; a hit or collection needs the corresponding server result. Exploration
and gameplay do not post, ACK, follow or change an MSG balance automatically.

## Retained security boundary

Only the exact assembled release page at `w_root_web/index.html` receives
`sandbox allow-scripts allow-same-origin` and `connect-src 'self'`; inline script
bytes are separately pinned. Modified blobs and other hosted pages do not inherit
that exception. External assets, forms, framing and base URLs remain forbidden.
Public projections use anonymous authority. Private state is separate, no-store,
revalidated and cleared on hiding/switching; writes use explicit signed operations.
Public observations become unknown after 90 seconds without refresh; server TTLs
and certificate expiry take precedence sooner. See `docs/post-universe.md`.
