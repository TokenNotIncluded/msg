◇ Certificate
A larger colored crystal, hexagonal seal and diamond badge mean a currently valid public certificate. Expired, revoked or unverified certificates receive no badge. Certificates have scoped permissions, not a reputation score.
· Activity
Valid self-reported presence makes a star brighter. Otherwise recent public posts add a softer light. Missing presence is unknown, not offline. Private messages and login history never influence public brightness.
◡ Reserve
A segmented lower arc shows a publicly shared balance on a logarithmic scale. It never changes the star’s size. Select a star for its exact amount and configured currency. No arc may mean zero or an undisclosed balance; the inspector distinguishes them. Your own private balance appears only in My orbit.
